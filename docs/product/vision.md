# Vision

Penka helps SVTEEHB teachers and their head of department follow the school year.

**Problem.** Progress through the national fiches de progression and the results of each séquence are tracked on paper. Preparing the Conseil d'Enseignement report takes hours of copying numbers.

**First version.**
- Teachers tick the lessons they have taught, against the official fiches (72 hours per year).
- After each séquence, teachers enter class counts: evaluated and ≥ 10, girls and boys.
- Penka computes success rates and coverage.
- The HOD sees all classes and gets a report "Rédigé par le système", ready to edit.
- Works offline; syncs when a connection is available.

**Not in the first version.** Student names, individual marks, other subjects.
