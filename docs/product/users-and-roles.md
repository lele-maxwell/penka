# Users and roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Enseignant | SVTEEHB teacher | Own classes only: track progression, enter séquence counts, see own statistics |
| Animateur pédagogique (HOD) | Head of the SVTEEHB department | Everything a teacher can, for all classes; generate and edit the CE report; manage teachers and classes |
