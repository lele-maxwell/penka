# Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SVTEEHB | Sciences de la Vie et de la Terre, Éducation à l'Environnement, Hygiène et Biotechnologie |
| Séquence | An assessment period; the school year has six |
| Fiche de progression | National list of lessons and hours for each class |
| CE | Conseil d'Enseignement, the department meeting; it gets a written report |
| HOD / Animateur pédagogique | Head of department |
| G / F | Garçons / Filles (boys / girls) |
| HP / HF / LP / LF / T | Abbreviations from the first-version spec (hours and lessons planned / done, total). See the spec for exact definitions. |
| APC | Approche par les compétences, the official teaching approach |
| Taux de réussite | Students with a mark ≥ 10 divided by students evaluated |
